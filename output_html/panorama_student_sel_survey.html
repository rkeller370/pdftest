We ask students these questions each time we conduct a survey, because we do not store the information anywhere we can access it. Your responses to these items will not be viewed individually, nor shared with your parents/guardians or teachers. School leaders look at patterns of responses in groups only, and use the information as one way to help them understand how students with various identify factors are experiencing our school environment. If fewer than 10 students select a specific choice, we cannot view response patterns by that particular group, in order to protect confidentiality.
