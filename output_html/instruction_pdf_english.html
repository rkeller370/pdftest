District 207 is now using ParentSquare as its school-to-home communication system. This platform is designed to keep parents and guardians informed and encourage greater engagement and connection with your child’s school.

Activating Your Account

Updating Language Settings

Most parents/guardians have activated their account already. However, if you have not activated your account, an email was sent today (August 28) with instructions on activating your account.

English is the default language for all messages and information ParentSquare. You can change your language preferences at any time after logging in:

On a computer, click your name at the top right corner > My Account > Language Settings. On the mobile app, click the three lines icon at the top left > Account > Preferences > Language.

Updating Notification Settings

You are able to choose how you would like to receive post notifications from ParentSquare. One logged in:

Click your name in the top right corner > My Account > Notification Settings. On the mobile app, click the three lines icon at the top left > Account > Notifications.
