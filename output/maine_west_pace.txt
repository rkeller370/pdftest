### MAINE WEST BUS ROUTE INFORMATION ###

Students should stand at a PACE bus sign along the route in order to be picked up. Buses only pick up at bus signs with the PACE logo and route number. There are bus stop locations all along these routes.

While there may be times when the school route is extremely late due to mechanical failure or other reasons, those routes always run.

These are approximate times not actual times - students should arrive at least five minutes early at the bus stop.

If the bus does not show up within 15 minutes of the posted times, please contact the school district at info@maine207.org with the date, time, location of the stop so that PACE can be notified.
